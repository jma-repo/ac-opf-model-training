Cmd 7a

Cost calibration

ran · kept illustrative costs

Before generating training data, the dispatch was checked against AESO metered unit output for 2024. Most of the gain came from fixing the model structure; fitting cost slopes by inverse optimisation did not beat the illustrative merit order, so those costs stay.

  • Load = AESO planning-area grid-served Load (excludes industrial self-supply).
  • Cogeneration, hydro and biomass pinned to metered output each hour; wind and solar availability = metered output.
  • Interchange = metered imports − exports, split across tie lines by rating.
  • Gas units: c1 = a + b·(1 − capacity factor) per fuel, (a, b) fitted with Nelder–Mead on 24 hours, checked with AC-OPF on 12 held-out hours.

Cmd 7b

How the training works, step by step

reference
1 data→2 graph→3 features→4 model→5 loss→6 train→7 evaluate
StepWhat happensCode
1 · DataEach scenario is solved by AC-OPF. The solver's answer (θ, V, Pg, Qg, branch flows) is the label; relaxation level 0 means feasible. 1,250 scenarios split into test 149, val 100, train pool 1,001, stratified by family.scenarios/generate.py, fm/finetune.py:splits
2 · GraphA scenario becomes a heterogeneous graph: bus, generator, load nodes, and line/transformer edges with per-unit features. Labels attach as bus.y, generator.y, edge_label, feasible.export/case.py, fm/dataset.py:load_labelled
3 · FeaturesFixed, not learned: branches turned into nodes, cycle basis, effective-resistance landmarks, DC power-flow features, bus-type one-hot, Hodge positional encodings (see Cmd 4).fm/dataset.py:make_transform
4 · ModelStart from the released GridSFM weights every round (no from-scratch network). 8 transformer blocks pass messages between node types; 5 heads predict θ, V, Pg, Qg and a feasibility logit.gridsfm.load_model
5 · LossSum of regression errors on θ, V, Pg, Qg and flows plus binary cross-entropy on feasibility. Infeasible graphs have no valid labels, so they only train the feasibility head.gridsfm compute_loss
6 · TrainMini-batches of 8 graphs, AdamW lr 1e-4. 20% of feasible graphs are perturbed into synthetic infeasible cases so the feasibility head sees enough negatives; real infeasible scenarios are added too. Validation loss each epoch.fm/dataset.py:train_dataset, gridsfm.finetune_opfdata
7 · EvaluateOn the untouched test split: MAE vs solver, cost recomputed from predicted Pg, feasibility accuracy, F1 on the infeasible class, calibration (ECE), time vs solver.fm/finetune.py:evaluate
